The eight-path external-token-install ownership proposal is reviewed and preserved
as source-only material in the published milestone. It has not been adopted,
formatted, compiled or tested. It does not complete credential storage ownership.

The established grouped focused build needs approximately 792,481,792 bytes of
currently absent outputs: core/TUI dependency pairs and login/CLI test executables.
This excludes growth, linker overlap and scratch. Full login/provider execution
adds approximately 248 MB. The current production CLI is 635,999,960 bytes; a new
auth-dependent CLI must coexist with preserved working artifacts during validation.
These are observed comparison sizes, not guaranteed upper bounds.

was about 992 MB against the previous focused-test admission floor of 2 GiB. The
16 GiB cgroup also charges tmpfs and shared-memory files. No compiler was launched
and no test failure was manufactured from a known insufficient budget.

executable. They could release 712,048,640 bytes of tmpfs after fresh content and
ownership checks, a preserved canonical payload/index, and complete restoration
proof. That does not increase overlay capacity, and its archive consumes overlay.
It cannot by itself admit the required build. No copies were retired. Source,
state, working binaries and all recovery archives remain intact. The plan records
inaccessible process-inspection entries rather than claiming global absence.
