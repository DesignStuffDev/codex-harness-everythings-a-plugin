# Native curated worker completion: scoped evidence

Final source commit `27d004ccd824b54a86400cee215af7ccd1148c76`, tree `2fa75acdf837420041e35d72ec2c157be1d0a37d`. Upstream stays pinned to `d42056091aded7feb1d88ac7e83972108b2aa478`. This is a native lifecycle prerequisite, not a separately installable component or a complete host-shutdown repair.

`PluginsManager::begin_curated_repo_sync_process_stop(deadline)` now irreversibly closes process-wide worker admission and returns `CuratedSyncStop`. Its `wait_until` observer distinguishes a handle awaiting attachment, running, joining, joined, panicked or failed to spawn. The original typed sync outcome remains separate: a joined quarantined worker stays quarantined, and `!is_pending()` alone is not success. Embedded server replacement must not invoke process-final closure.

An unfinished handle stays owned by the gate when an observer expires, is cancelled or is dropped. Competing native observers share one exact join. A retry already joining when stop wins retains its original record. Joining and panic-payload destruction occur outside the gate mutex; there is no await between taking a finished handle and publishing its join result. The polling deadline is **not a hard wall-clock bound**: native TLS teardown, a synchronous join or arbitrary panic-payload destruction can exceed it. No detached drain or independent force/watchdog policy is introduced.

Seven new tests cover idle closure; outcome before handle attachment; cancelled/expired observers during native teardown; two concurrent native observers; spawn failure after closure; post-outcome native panic with reentrant payload destruction; and Linux TLS-controlled stop during retry join. Existing curated sync, HTTP policy and process ownership regressions remain in the suite.
